**For:**

- The product is technically deployable and the founders can execute in the field: a working 10-manhole opioid monitoring pilot ran in Cary, NC.
- It offers a differentiated upstream, neighbourhood-level data type, aimed at a crisis that mayors rank as a top priority.
- There is a credible long-run platform story: dashboards, an API and alerts per 2026 sources, plus a granted 2023 hardware patent.
- The team showed intellectual honesty under diligence (withdrew the unsourced price, conceded IP limits), which lowers governance risk.

**Against:**

- There is no evidenced paying customer. The four lead cities are targets only, and the Cary pilot has no price or terms on record.
- The buyer and budget line are unknown, and the relevant Boston line is small (FY18 $509,108) and shrank about 25% from FY16.
- Sourced market sizing is small: about $1.07M (71 cities) to $13M (870 cities) at the only sourced price, the $15,000 minimum. That is venture-scale only if large-city pricing is far higher, which is unproven.
- The moat is weak. Sampling hardware was off-the-shelf, the testing procedures were left unprotected, and the patent covers narrow hardware, not the analytics.
- Unit economics are entirely unmeasured: lab, hardware, shipping and reporting costs are unknown against a thin price anchor.

**Terms:** Pass at this time. I would revisit a small seed (e.g. $500k–$1M SAFE with a modest cap) only after proof of a paid contract. Alternatively, a tranched structure: $250k now, with the remainder released on (a) a signed paid contract with a named budget holder at ≥$15k/yr, and (b) a measured per-sample cost from a 10-site deployment showing positive gross margin.

**Would change my mind:**

- A signed, paid contract or funded LOI from at least one of NYC, Boston, SF or DC, naming the signing office and budget line.
- Measured cost per sample and per site from a 10-site deployment, showing ≥50% gross margin at realistic pricing.
- Evidence that large cities will pay well above the $15,000 minimum (e.g. ≥$100k/yr), which would expand the 71-city market to venture scale.
- A renewal or repeat purchase showing recurring rather than one-off pilot revenue.
- Evidence of a customer choosing Biobot over a lab or utility doing it in-house, or proprietary data or network effects that competitors cannot replicate.
